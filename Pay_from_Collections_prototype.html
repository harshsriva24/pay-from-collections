24x7 Help
A payment failed. When does my money come back?›
A failed UPI payment is reversed to your own bank account automatically, usually within minutes. Under RBI rules the bank must reverse it by the next working day.
Does Paytm keep my money?›
No. Your collection settles to your bank exactly as today. When you pay a supplier, the money goes from that bank account by UPI.
My supplier uses PhonePe. Will the payment reach?›
Yes. UPI works across every app and bank. Scan their QR or pay their number from here.
Why do I need a PIN for every staff payment?›
UPI rules need your PIN for each payment. Payments up to ₹1,000 can go by UPI Lite without a PIN.
